# 7 Wonders (2nd Edition) — Rules

> This is a concise, original-language rules reference for the base game. It is not a reproduction of the publisher's rulebook. See the official rulebook linked below for the complete rules, examples, and card details.

- **Players:** 3–7
- **Ages:** 10+
- **Playing time:** about 30 minutes
- **Source:** [Official rules and game aids — Repos Production](https://www.rprod.com/en/games/7-wonders) (English rules under “Downloads”)

## Goal

Earn the most victory points by developing your city over three Ages. Points can come from civilian buildings, military victories, science, commerce, guilds, your Wonder, and remaining coins.

## Setup

1. Each player takes a Wonder board and chooses its Day or Night side. Place it in front of them.
2. Each player takes 3 coins.
3. Sort the Age I, II, and III cards by player count using the symbols on the cards. Return unused cards to the box.
4. Shuffle each Age deck separately. Set aside the Guild cards for Age III, then randomly add a number of Guild cards equal to the number of players plus 2. Each Age deck should now contain 7 cards per player.
5. Put the Military Conflict tokens and remaining coins within reach.

## Playing an Age

At the start of each Age, deal 7 cards to each player. Each Age has six regular turns. Players act simultaneously:

1. Each player takes their Age hand.
2. Choose one card and keep it hidden.
3. Reveal together and choose one action:
   - **Build the structure:** Pay its cost, then place it face up in your city.
   - **Build a Wonder stage:** Put the chosen card face down beneath the next unfinished stage of your Wonder and pay that stage's cost. The card's printed effect is not used.
   - **Discard for coins:** Discard the card face down and take 3 coins from the bank.
4. Pass the remaining hand to the next player. Pass left during Ages I and III, and right during Age II.

On the sixth turn, each player receives two cards. Choose one for an action and discard the other face down. The final card is not passed onward and gives no coins. Some Wonder abilities change what you may do with that last card; follow their effects.

You cannot build two structures with the same name in your city. Wonder stages are optional and must be built from left to right; they do not need to match the current Age. Your Wonder provides its printed starting resource even before you build a stage.

## Paying for construction

A card or Wonder stage may require resources. Use resources produced by your brown and gray buildings and any applicable yellow-card or Wonder abilities.

Resources are production capacity: using them does not consume them, and each symbol can be used once per turn. A choice between resources supplies one of them that turn.

If you are short of resources, you may buy them from either neighboring city. Each resource bought costs 2 coins by default. Trade abilities may reduce the price or change which resources you can buy. You may buy resources from both neighbors in the same turn. A resource produced by a neighbor can be bought even if that neighbor is also using it to build.

You can buy only a neighbor’s starting resource and resources on brown or gray cards. Resources from their yellow cards or built Wonder stages cannot be purchased. Each production symbol can be bought once per turn by each neighbor; the producer can still use it too. Newly built production is available starting on the next turn. Bought resources must be used immediately and cannot be stored.

Coins gained during a turn cannot be spent until a later turn. If a card's cost can be paid through a chain symbol shown on one of your previously built structures, build it for free.

## Military conflicts

At the end of each Age, compare each player's total shield symbols with each of their two neighbors. For each comparison:

- Higher military strength wins a Conflict token: **+1 point** for Age I, **+3 points** for Age II, or **+5 points** for Age III.
- Lower military strength takes a Defeat token worth **−1 point**.
- Tied strength gives neither player a token.

Resolve both neighbors independently. Military strength comes from red cards and any other effects that provide shields.

## Science

Green cards show one of three scientific symbols: compass, tablet, or gear.

- Each set of three different symbols is worth **7 points**.
- Each symbol scores its count squared: one symbol type appearing *n* times is worth *n²* points.
- Add these scores together. Include symbols from Wonder stages and other applicable effects. Each wildcard represents one symbol of your choice at scoring; choose the combination giving the highest total.

## End of game and scoring

After the Age III military conflicts, total each player's points:

- **Blue civilian buildings:** printed points.
- **Wonder:** points and other scoring effects from completed stages.
- **Military:** Conflict tokens, including losses.
- **Science:** score the symbols as described above.
- **Commercial buildings:** points from their effects.
- **Guilds:** points according to each guild's effect.
- **Treasury:** 1 point for every 3 coins left, rounded down.

The player with the most points wins. If tied, the tied player with the most coins wins; if still tied, the players share the victory.

## Card colors at a glance

| Color | Usual role |
|---|---|
| Brown | Produces raw materials |
| Gray | Produces manufactured goods |
| Blue | Scores civilian points |
| Yellow | Provides trade, coins, or other commercial effects |
| Red | Adds military shields |
| Green | Provides science symbols |
| Purple | Guilds score based on conditions described on the card |
